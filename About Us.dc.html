About Us

Built for pickleball players in Cebu — quality courts, easy booking and a place where anyone can walk in and find a game.

OUR STORY

A court you can count on. A crowd you'll love.

Pickleball took off in Cebu fast — faster than the places to play it. Players were squeezing games onto borrowed basketball courts and chasing last-minute slots.

So we built a facility just for the game: four quality courts, simple online booking, and room to park, rest and hang out between rallies.

Whether it's your first dink or your hundredth tournament, you'll find a court ready and a community happy to play.
